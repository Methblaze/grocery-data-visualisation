Grocery Shopping Data Visualisation

Three weeks of grocery receipts from five people, laid out as the shop they were bought in. Each aisle is a category. Look at one person's store, where shading shows where their money went, or compare people aisle by aisle.

Hover an aisle for the exact amount. The two counters at the bottom are the checkout and the discounts. Shading is scaled within the person shown, so it compares aisles rather than people. The wiper sweeps between this version and the one submitted in 2024. Scroll the store sideways to see all of it.

Each aisle holds one bar per person, all on the same scale, so bars can be read against each other both inside an aisle and across the store. Pick people above to add or remove them. Scroll the store sideways to see all of it.

Course project at ITU, built with D3. Data collected by the group from its own receipts between 20 March and 10 April 2024. Interface redesigned in 2026.
